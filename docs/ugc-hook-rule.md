# Ugc Hook Rule

Clip 1 of every UGC script is the hook: livelier than the body clips but NATURAL, not performed. Direction (Oct 24 latest, from [ugc-delivery-reference](ugc-delivery-reference.md)): lit up like she just found something great, eyebrows up, eyes wide on the key word, a real smile breaking through, head tilt into the camera, free hand already gesturing at chest height; genuinely enthusiastic and human, not a presenter, not performing; fast fluent pace with a tiny beat after the key phrase; voice natural and warm, lightly upbeat, slightly imperfect like a real voice memo. The first version ("big real smile, animated, excited, energetic") was rejected on Oct 24 as "too expressive, sounds and feels AI". The pipeline (ugc_pipeline.py) does this automatically for clip 1 via HOOK_MOOD / HOOK_PACE / HOOK_AUDIO and uses clip 1 negatives: overacting, performing, big grin, exaggerated expression, presenter voice, salesy, robotic (plus the standard list). Clips 2+ keep the relaxed FaceTime delivery.

**Why:** the user asked on Oct 24 2026 that the hook always be exciting and expressive, after a flat first clip on the "cheat code for engineers" script.
**How to apply:** keep the hook lively but human; never push it to excited/performed again, and never fall back to the flat relaxed wording either. Body clips also carry the "slightly imperfect like a real voice memo, not a presenter read" audio note. Part of [ugc-workflow](ugc-workflow.md).
