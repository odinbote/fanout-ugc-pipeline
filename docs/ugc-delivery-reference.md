# Ugc Delivery Reference

Reference clip: fanout 02 ugc ide/Video-1174.mp4 (41 s, 720x1280). The user shared it on Oct 24 2026 saying "see how her hand movement is there and how she says and her expression", person only, ignore the edit.

**Hands:** one hand holds a small mic at chest height the whole time; the free hand gestures almost continuously at chest/shoulder height, never still for more than ~1 s: open-palm "flat hand" emphasis, fingers spread on numbers, a quick point on "not a / no", small circular and sweeping motions while listing, both hands briefly up on big beats. Gestures land on the stressed word.

**Face:** big, readable expressions that change every phrase: eyebrows jump on surprises and numbers, eyes widen on "no / just once / gone", mouth opens wide on stressed vowels, eyes narrow/squint on sly lines, brief eye closes on emphasis, head tilts and nods with the rhythm, a real smile on the close. Eyes come back to the lens between every look-away.

**Voice:** ~4.5 words/s, rapid-fire with deliberate micro-pauses after the key phrase ("Just once." / before the closer), strong upward inflections on surprises and downward on finals, contrastive stress ("not a factory, not a sales target"), high energy but casual, like a friend who just found something, no fillers, no laugh, slightly imperfect not a teleprompter read.

**How to apply:** this is the target the user means by "expressive like a human" and "pacing": continuous natural hand gestures that hit the stressed words, expressive face that changes per phrase, fast fluent delivery with micro-pauses after key phrases.
